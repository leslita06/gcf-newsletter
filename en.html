Dieu n'est pas obligé

Roméo Mivekannin
5 November 2026 – 9 January 2027
Galerie Cécile Fakhoury · Cocody, boulevard Latrille, Abidjan
 

Dieu n'est pas obligé (God Is Not Obliged) is Roméo Mivekannin's third solo exhibition in Côte d'Ivoire. Through a pantheon of rituals and images, the artist pursues a quest between West Africa, Europe and Brazil, shaped by expressions of the sacred, the metamorphosis of gestures and the ambivalence of religion.

Painted mostly in white acrylic on unstretched black velvet, his figures emerge from the night: initiations, sacrifices, dances. The title echoes Ahmadou Kourouma's novel Allah n'est pas obligé.
